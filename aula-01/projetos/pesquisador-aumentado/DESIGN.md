## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

Pesquisador Aumentado™ sells a serious idea to a serious audience — Brazilian TCC advisors weighing whether AI belongs in academic supervision — and the visual system is built to argue that seriousness without raising its voice. The page's own CSS comment states the intent plainly: "produto de IA sério e sereno (anti-hype)" (a serious, serene AI product — anti-hype). Everything downstream follows from that brief: a warm cream canvas (`#faf9f5`) instead of clinical white, an almost-black ink (`#141413`) instead of pure `#000000`, and a single terracotta accent (`#d97757`) deployed like a wax seal — never as a fill, always as a mark of provenance.

Typography carries most of the personality. The body copy runs at a generous 1.12rem/1.68 line-height — roomy, essay-like, built for long-form reading rather than scanning — while headlines snap tight with negative letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) for a precise, engineered edge. A single monospace family (`ui-monospace, SF Mono, Roboto Mono...`) is reserved exclusively for uppercase micro-labels — eyebrows, part numbers, the signature citation — giving the page a technical, almost lab-notebook register wherever it needs to signal precision. A serif face (`Iowan Old Style, Palatino Linotype, Georgia...`) appears only twice, both times as italicized editorial blockquotes, functioning like a pull-quote in a print essay rather than a UI element.

The surface treatment is deliberately flat: there is no `box-shadow` value declared anywhere in the stylesheet, no `backdrop-filter`, no gradient. Depth is communicated entirely through a single hairline border token (`rgba(20,20,19,0.10)`) and background-color banding (`.band`, `.band-ink`) — a restraint that reads as confidence rather than austerity. The single most distinctive choice on the page is the terracotta accent's discipline: it never fills a surface, it only marks — a 2px rule under section eyebrows, a 3px left border on quoted ledes, a focus ring, a single highlighted letter in the wordmark. It behaves exactly like a brand mark should: rare, consistent, unmistakable.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Warm cream canvas (`#faf9f5`) + near-black ink (`#141413`) — never pure white or pure black
- Terracotta accent (`#d97757`) used only as a stroke/mark — logo highlight, rule-mark, focus ring, eyebrow labels — never as a fill
- Zero shadows, zero blur — depth is border + background-band contrast only
- Generous body line-height (1.68) paired with tight, negative-tracked display type (-0.02em to -0.03em)
- Monospace reserved exclusively for uppercase micro-labels — a technical register layered over an editorial one
- Serif italics appear only in two pull-quotes — an editorial accent, not a body font
- A single button style throughout — no secondary/outline/ghost variant; emphasis comes from copy and placement, not chrome
- 40px minimum button height, explicitly commented in source as "alvo mínimo de toque" (minimum touch target)
- No product screenshots or illustrations anywhere — the entire page is typography, whitespace, and one accent color

## 2. Color Palette & Roles

### Primary

- **Clay Terracotta** (`#d97757`): `--ring`. The brand's identity mark. Appears in the wordmark's highlighted glyph, the eyebrow labels above every section, the 2px `.rule-mark` divider, the focus-visible ring on every interactive element, and the `blockquote` left-border accent. Used sparingly and consistently — never as a background fill.

### Brand & Dark

- **Ink** (`#141413`): `--primary` / `--foreground`. Doubles as base body text color and the fill for the dark "band-ink" section (`.signature`), where it inverts to host cream-colored text. This is the dominant *action* color — every CTA button uses it — even though it is not the identity mark.
- **Cream on Ink**: `--primary-fg` (`#faf9f5`) is the text/foreground color used on top of ink surfaces (buttons, dark band).

### Accent Colors

- **Destructive Rust** (`#c6613f`): `--destructive`. A darker, redder cousin of the terracotta accent — the palette deliberately keeps its "alert" color in the same warm family rather than introducing a clashing red.

### Interactive

- **Hover** (`rgba(0,0,0,0.08)` → effective `#e6e5e1` composited over canvas): `--state-hover`. Declared but not directly wired to a component in this build — the live button hover instead uses `opacity: .90`, which against the ink fill computes to `#2b2b2a`.
- **Active/Pressed** (`rgba(0,0,0,0.16)` → effective `#d2d1ce`): `--state-pressed`. Live `.btn:active` uses `opacity: .82`, computing to `#3d3d3c` against ink.
- **Focus** (`--ring`, `#d97757`): every focus-visible state — buttons and links alike — uses a 2px terracotta outline with 3px offset. This is the single most consistent interactive signature on the page.

### Neutral Scale

- **Foreground** (`#141413`): `--foreground`. Primary text, headings, strong emphasis.
- **Muted Foreground** (`#5e5d59`): `--fg-muted`. Secondary text — subheads, list descriptions, fine print, footer copy.

### Surface & Borders

- **Canvas** (`#faf9f5`): `--canvas`. Root page background and the sticky masthead background — warm cream, not white.
- **Card** (`#ffffff`): `--card`. Reserved for the single elevated surface on the page — the price card — providing contrast against the cream canvas without introducing a shadow.
- **Secondary Band** (`#f0eee6`): `--secondary-bg`. Used exclusively as a background wash for alternating `.band` sections; the source comment also flags it as an intended "botão secundário" (secondary button) color, though no secondary button is actually implemented in this build.
- **Border** (`rgba(20,20,19,0.10)`, effective `#e3e2de` composited over canvas): `--border`. Hairline dividers — masthead bottom edge, list item separators, card outline.
- **Border Strong** (`rgba(20,20,19,0.18)`, effective `#d1d0cc` composited over canvas): `--border-strong`. Used once, for the dashed guarantee-box border.

### Shadow Colors

None. The system declares no `box-shadow` value anywhere (only a transition hook on the property, never triggered). Depth is entirely a function of the border and band tokens above.

### Color Philosophy

The palette reads as a deliberate rebellion against SaaS-default blue: no link-blue, no gradient, no saturated brand color used at scale. Warm neutrals do the heavy lifting so that when terracotta appears — always as a thin mark, never a fill — it reads as intentional punctuation rather than decoration. This mirrors the page's own thesis: an AI tool that "questions the student instead of answering for them" is expressed visually by a palette that restrains itself instead of shouting.

## 3. Typography Rules

### Font Family

The system uses **no self-hosted webfonts** — every role resolves to a native system-font stack (`system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`), split conceptually into `--font-display` and `--font-body` even though both currently resolve to the identical stack. A dedicated monospace stack (`ui-monospace, "SF Mono", "Roboto Mono", "JetBrains Mono", "Consolas", monospace`) is reserved for uppercase micro-labels, and a serif stack (`"Iowan Old Style", "Palatino Linotype", Georgia, "Times New Roman", serif`) is reserved for the two editorial blockquotes. No OpenType feature flags (`ss01`, `tnum`, etc.) are declared anywhere in the stylesheet.

### Principles

- Display roles (`h1`, `h2`, `h3`, `.price-amount`) always carry negative letter-spacing (-0.01em to -0.03em) — the deeper the emphasis, the tighter the tracking.
- Every uppercase mono label (`eyebrow`, `part-label`, `cita`, `masthead-note`) carries *positive* letter-spacing (0.12em–0.18em) — tracking direction inverts between display type and label type, and that inversion is the system's signature typographic rule.
- Body copy runs unusually roomy — 1.12rem base size at 1.68 line-height — favoring long-form legibility over UI density; this is an editorial reading experience, not a dashboard.
- The serif face is never used for structure, only for two italicized pull-quotes (`.ethics blockquote`, `.signature blockquote`) — treat it as an editorial accent, not a heading alternative.
- `.btn` text sits at weight 500, one step below the 700 used for headings — buttons never compete visually with headline type.
- No OpenType features (`tnum`, `ss01`, etc.) are enabled anywhere; do not add font-feature-settings when generating UI in this system.

## 5. Layout Principles

### Spacing System

No systematic 4px/8px spacing scale is declared — spacing is authored ad hoc in `rem`, but a coherent scale can be read from the recurring values: `2xs` 3px (blockquote padding), `xs` 8px (list margins), `sm` 16px (masthead gaps), `md` 24px (`.wrap` horizontal padding), `lg` 38px (audience grid gap / footer padding), `xl` 72px (`--gap-section`, the vertical rhythm between every major section), `xxl` 80px (hero top padding).

### Grid & Container

- Body copy container (`.wrap`): `max-width: 34rem` (544px, `--measure`) — an explicitly narrow reading column.
- Masthead container: `max-width: 62rem` (992px) — wider than the body column, giving the nav room to breathe.
- Hero container: `max-width: 44rem` (704px) — a middle width for the headline.
- Two-column grid only appears once: `.audience { grid-template-columns: 1fr 1fr; gap: 2.4rem }` (the "para quem é / para quem não é" comparison), collapsing to a single column under 640px.

### Whitespace Philosophy

Every section is separated by `--gap-section` (4.5rem / 72px desktop, 3.4rem / 54.4px mobile) — a single, page-wide vertical rhythm token rather than per-section tuning. Combined with the narrow 34rem reading column and the 1.68 body line-height, the page reads more like a printed essay with generous margins than a conversion-optimized landing page. Whitespace here is doing the same rhetorical work as the flat, shadow-free surfaces: it signals that the content doesn't need to compete for attention.

### Border Radius Scale

- `none` (0px) — default sharp corners, implied by the box-reset
- `sm` (2px) — link focus-visible outline radius
- `md` (4px) — buttons, inputs (`--radius-button`, `--radius-input`)
- `lg` (8px) — cards, guarantee box (`--radius-card`)
- `full` (9999px) — the single decorative rule-mark bar only (`--radius-pill`)

## 6. Depth & Elevation

| Level | Treatment | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Flat | No border, no shadow | Body text, running copy |
| Ambient | 1px hairline border (`#e3e2de`) | List item dividers, masthead bottom edge |
| Standard | 1px solid border + white surface | Price card |
| Elevated | *(not used)* | — this system defines no elevated/shadowed surface |
| Deep | *(not used)* | — no modal/overlay shadow tokens exist |
| Ring (focus) | 2px solid `#d97757`, 3px offset | Every focus-visible state — buttons and links |

### Shadow Philosophy

The system is intentionally low-elevation: zero `box-shadow` declarations exist anywhere in the stylesheet, and no `backdrop-filter` is used. Depth is communicated exclusively through border-hairline contrast and background-color banding (`.band`, `.band-ink`, `.price-card`'s white-on-cream lift). This is a deliberate anti-hype choice consistent with the brand's stated positioning — a shadow-heavy, glassy UI would undercut the "sério e sereno" (serious and serene) tone the source CSS explicitly calls for.

## 7. Do's and Don'ts

- ✅ Do use `#141413` ink, not pure `#000000`, for all text and dark surfaces — the near-black is part of the warm palette, and pure black will read as a foreign, colder note against the cream canvas.
- ✅ Do reserve `#d97757` terracotta for marks only — eyebrows, rule-marks, focus rings, the wordmark highlight — never as a button or card fill.
- ✅ Do keep negative letter-spacing (-0.02em to -0.03em) on all display type, and positive letter-spacing (0.12em–0.18em) on all uppercase mono labels — the direction inversion is this system's typographic signature.
- ✅ Do use the serif stack only for italicized pull-quotes with a 3px terracotta left border — it is an editorial accent, never a heading font.
- ❌ Don't add `box-shadow` to cards or buttons — this system has zero shadow declarations; a shadowed card will look like it belongs to a different product.
- ❌ Don't introduce a second button style (outline, ghost, ring) — the source defines exactly one `.btn` class; new emphasis should come from copy and placement, not new button chrome.
- ❌ Don't use `#f0eee6` (`--secondary-bg`) as a button fill — despite its name and source comment ("botão secundário"), it is only ever applied to section-band backgrounds in this build.
- ❌ Don't round buttons or inputs beyond `4px`, and never apply the `9999px` pill radius to anything but the decorative rule-mark bar.
- ❌ Don't add `backdrop-filter`/glass blur to the sticky masthead — it uses a solid `#faf9f5` fill with a 1px bottom hairline, not translucency.
- ❌ Don't introduce a saturated red or green for error/success states — the source keeps `--destructive` (`#c6613f`) inside the same warm terracotta family as the brand accent rather than a clashing hue.

## 8. Responsive Behavior

### Breakpoints

| Name | Width | Key Changes |
|---|---|---|
| Desktop (default) | > 640px | Full type scale, two-column audience grid, `--gap-section: 4.5rem`, masthead note visible |
| Mobile | ≤ 640px | Type scale downshifts, single-column audience grid, `--gap-section: 3.4rem`, masthead note hidden |

Only one breakpoint is declared in the stylesheet (`@media (max-width: 640px)`); everything above it is handled by the fluid, max-width-constrained containers (`.wrap`, `.hero .wrap`, `.masthead-inner`) rather than additional breakpoints.

### Touch Targets

- Minimum button height: `2.5rem` (40px) — explicitly commented in the source CSS as "alvo mínimo de toque" (minimum touch target).
- Focus-visible outline offset (`3px`) is preserved at all sizes, including mobile, so keyboard/switch-access users retain a visible ring regardless of viewport.

### Collapsing Strategy

- `.hero h1`: 2.55rem → 1.95rem
- `.hero .lede`: 1.26rem → 1.12rem
- `h2`: 1.9rem → 1.5rem
- `.price-amount`: 3.2rem → 2.6rem
- `.signature blockquote`: 1.35rem → 1.15rem
- `.audience` grid: `1fr 1fr` → single column, gap 2.4rem → 2rem
- `body` base size: 1.12rem → 1.05rem
- `.masthead-note`: hidden entirely (`display: none`) below 640px
- `--gap-section` (the page-wide vertical rhythm token itself): 4.5rem → 3.4rem

### Image Behavior

This is a **text-only page** — no `<img>`, background image, or illustration appears anywhere in the markup or CSS. Visual hierarchy and pacing are carried entirely by typography, the `--gap-section` rhythm, and the terracotta accent marks. If imagery is introduced later, it should follow the `.wrap` (34rem) or `.hero .wrap` (44rem) container widths rather than breaking full-bleed, to stay consistent with the page's narrow, essay-like column discipline.

### Iteration Guide

1. Always use `#141413` for ink, never `#000000` — this system's black has warmth baked in.
2. Reserve `#d97757` for marks, not fills — a button, card, or band filled solid terracotta breaks the brand's core discipline.
3. Tighten letter-spacing on anything larger than 1.18rem (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`); loosen it on anything set in the mono uppercase label style (`0.12em` to `0.18em`). Never mix the two directions on the same element.
4. Keep body copy roomy: 1.12rem at 1.68 line-height is the system's default reading rhythm — don't tighten it to typical 1.4–1.5 UI line-heights.
5. Never add `box-shadow` or `backdrop-filter` — depth comes only from the `#e3e2de` hairline border and background-color banding.
6. There is one button style. Resist the urge to add an outline/ghost/secondary variant — express hierarchy through copy weight and placement instead.
7. Buttons stay at `4px` radius; the `9999px` pill token belongs only to the decorative rule-mark bar, never to buttons, cards, or badges.
8. Use the serif stack (`Iowan Old Style...`) only for italicized quotes with a `#d97757` left border — treat it as punctuation, not a heading alternative.
